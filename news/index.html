New Preprint: Freehand Sketching for Robot Swarms

The sketch-to-swarm preprint is now on arXiv! “Freehand Sketching for End-User Programming of Robot Swarms” explores how a simple drawing can guide a group of robots. May 20, 2026

New Preprint: Learning Altruistic Collaboration

Shared a new preprint on arXiv: “Learning Altruistic Collaboration in Heterogeneous Multi-Team Systems.” The starting question is simple: when should one team give a robot to another? The answer accounts for different capabilities, team contributions, and transfer costs, using Hamilton’s rule to guide the decision. A graph neural network helps tackle the NP-hard allocation problem at larger scales, with firefighting scenarios in simulation and on physical robots to put the approach to the test.

March 25, 2026

New Preprint: Multi-Robot Collaboration Survey

The multi-robot collaboration survey is on arXiv! “Collaboration in Multi-Robot Systems: Taxonomy and Survey of Frameworks for Collaboration” takes a closer look at three words that often get grouped together: cooperation, coordination, and collaboration. Robot examples help explain the differences, especially when working together creates new capabilities. The survey also brings together collaboration frameworks, open challenges, and possible next directions.
