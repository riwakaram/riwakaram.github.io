About

Robotics research grounded in practical experience

Fourth-year PhD candidate in the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science Department at the University of California, Irvine. Member of the UCI Robot Ecology Lab and part of the Center for Resilient Autonomous Systems at UCI, advised by Professors Magnus Egerstedt and Yanning Shen. Exploring collaborative multi-agent systems, optimization, machine learning, ecology robotics, coverage control, formation control, and human-swarm interaction.

Earned a B.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Balamand, graduating as valedictorian, followed by an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Irvine. Bringing experience from software engineering and mechatronics research, including two internships at ASML focused on high-precision systems, statistical modeling, and Bayesian machine learning.
